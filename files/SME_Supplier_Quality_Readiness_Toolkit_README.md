# SME Supplier Quality Readiness Toolkit

Version 1.0 · 23 September 2026 · Amarachukwu (Amara) Tasie

This toolkit helps small and medium-sized U.S. manufacturers prepare a new or changed part for customer review, monitor a supplier, and investigate a quality problem. It is a set of original planning aids. It is not an AIAG publication, a PPAP approval, a certification, or a substitute for customer, regulatory, or sector requirements.

## Files

- `SME_Supplier_Quality_Readiness_Toolkit_Workbook.xlsx` contains the APQP Timeline, PPAP Checklist, Supplier Scorecard, and Start Here instructions.
- `SME_8D_Corrective_Action_Guide.docx` explains D0 through D8 and provides a working corrective action record.

## Start a launch

1. Make a copy of the workbook for each part or launch. Record the drawing revision, customer requirements, and launch owner.
2. On **APQP Timeline**, enter a kickoff date and adjust the proposed week offsets and owners. The target date formula uses `kickoff date + 7 × (start week + duration weeks)`. These are planning defaults. Agree actual milestones with the customer.
3. On **PPAP Checklist**, mark what applies only after checking the customer contract and applicable manuals. Record the acceptance rule, evidence path, status, owner, and customer decision. A `No with customer agreement` entry needs the actual agreement. An internally completed package is not a customer approval.
4. Run the intended process at its intended rate. Keep traceable evidence that the controls work and escalate open special-characteristic, safety, or regulatory risks.
5. Review launch defects and escapes after approval. If a failure occurs, use the 8D guide to contain it, test both the occurrence and escape causes, and validate the permanent action.

## Use the scorecard

Enter a supplier and period, then source counts: on-time shipments and shipments due, rejected and inspected units, actions closed on time and actions due, and accepted and due evidence items. The calculated percentages and score update from those counts. The default weights are delivery 30%, quality 40%, corrective action timeliness 20%, and evidence 10%; edit them to reflect your priorities and keep their sum at 100%. If no actions or evidence items were due, leave both corresponding cells blank. The score reweights the dimensions that have data. A missing required dimension must be investigated before comparison. The 85% investigation trigger is an editable internal example, not a customer acceptance limit.

Compare suppliers only when the product mix and inspection coverage make comparison meaningful. Preserve source records. A weighted score never overrides a safety concern, a stop-ship decision, or contractual approval requirements.
