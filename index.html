How to begin

1. Agree requirements

Confirm the drawing revision, customer submission rules, acceptance criteria, and decision maker.

2. Plan and prove the process

Assign launch gates, connect risks to controls, and keep records from the intended production conditions.

3. Obtain the decision

Submit the customer-agreed evidence. Record the actual approval, then monitor early production.

Scope and source notes

These are original planning aids. They do not replace the current AIAG manuals, a customer-required PPAP package, customer approval, or safety and regulatory obligations. No pilot performance claim or company data are included.
